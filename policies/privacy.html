# Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Who we are

Final Judgment is run by Tommaso Cristoni, a sole trader in England, and is the controller of your personal data. Contact: iudiciumfinale@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • When you join our list: your email address and, if you give it, your mobile number.
  • When you order: your name, delivery address, email, phone and order details. Card payments are handled by Stripe; we never see your full card number.
  • When you browse: cookies and similar technology from Meta (Facebook and Instagram) to measure our ads, if you allow them.

Why we use it

  • To send you drop news by email and text, only if you signed up (your consent). You can unsubscribe from any email, or reply STOP to any text.
  • To process and deliver your order and handle returns (to perform our contract with you).
  • To measure and improve our ads (our legitimate interest, or your consent where the law requires it).

Who we share it with

  • Klaviyo, which sends our emails and texts.
  • FormSubmit, which forwards new sign-ups to our inbox until our mailing system is live.
  • Stripe, which processes payments.
  • Meta, for ad measurement.
  • Delivery carriers, to ship your order.

We never sell your data.

How long we keep it

List data until you unsubscribe. Order records for 6 years, for tax reasons.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or object to how we use it, by emailing iudiciumfinale@gmail.com. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
