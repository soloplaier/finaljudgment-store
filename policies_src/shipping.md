# Shipping Policy

Last updated: November 2026

### Pre-orders
Pre-order items are made to order after the pre-order window closes. The current drop is open from 25 December 2026 to 8 January 2027, and our target is to ship in late February 2027. This date is a target, not a guarantee. If it moves, we will email you with the new date, and you can still cancel for a full refund at any time before your order ships.

If your order contains both pre-order and in-stock items, we ship everything together when the pre-order items are ready, unless you ask us to split it.

### In-stock items
In-stock orders are dispatched within 3 working days.

### Customs and import charges
Orders sent outside the UK may be charged import duties, taxes or handling fees when they arrive. These are set by your country and are paid by you to the carrier. We cannot change the declared value of an order.

### Incorrect addresses
Please check your address carefully at checkout. We are not responsible for orders sent to an incorrect or incomplete address entered by the customer, and we do not refund or replace them. If a parcel comes back to us for this reason, or because it was not collected, we can send it again once you pay the new delivery cost.

### Delayed or missing parcels
Once an order has been dispatched, delivery is handled by the carrier. If your tracking shows no movement for 10 working days, email us and we will open an investigation with the carrier. We only replace or refund an order once the carrier has confirmed it as lost. Parcels shown as delivered by the carrier's tracking are treated as delivered.
