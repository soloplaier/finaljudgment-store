# Returns & Refunds

Last updated: November 2026

### Cancelling a pre-order
You can cancel a pre-order at any time before it ships for a full refund. Email us with your order number and we will refund you within 14 days.

### Changed your mind?
If you are a customer in the UK or EU, you have 14 days from the day you receive your order to tell us you want to return it, for any reason. You then have a further 14 days to send it back.

- Items must be unworn, unwashed and in their original condition with tags attached. If an item has been used beyond what is needed to check it, we may reduce the refund to reflect that.
- You pay the cost of returning the item, unless it is faulty or not as described.
- We refund the price you paid and the standard delivery charge within 14 days of receiving the item back (or proof you have sent it).
- To start a return, email iudiciumfinale@gmail.com with your order number. We will reply with the return address.

### Faulty, damaged or wrong items
If your item arrives faulty, damaged or not as described, email us within 30 days of delivery with your order number and a photo. We will offer a replacement or a full refund, including delivery costs. This does not affect your legal rights.

### Sizing
Please check the size guide on each product page before ordering. If the size is not right, you can return it under the 14-day right above. We do not offer free exchanges, but you are welcome to return the item and place a new order.

### How refunds are paid
Refunds go back to the original payment method. Your bank may take a few working days to show it.

### Questions or problems
Please contact us before opening a dispute with your bank. We reply within 3 working days.
